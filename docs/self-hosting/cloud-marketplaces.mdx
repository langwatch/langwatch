Yes, you can buy LangWatch through **Azure Marketplace** and **AWS Marketplace**. The purchase is billed to your cloud account, so it goes through the vendor and procurement process you already have with Microsoft or AWS.

## Where to buy

| Marketplace | How to buy |
| --- | --- |
| **Azure Marketplace** | [LangWatch on Azure Marketplace](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/langwatch.langwatch), or a private offer with custom terms |
| **AWS Marketplace** | A private offer with custom terms: [contact sales](https://langwatch.ai/get-a-demo) |

## Private offers

A private offer is a listing made for your organization only: your price, seat count, contract length and terms, accepted in your own marketplace account. Enterprise deals of any size can go through one.

To get one, [contact sales](https://langwatch.ai/get-a-demo) or email [enterprise@langwatch.ai](mailto:enterprise@langwatch.ai). Tell us which marketplace you use and the account or tenant that will accept the offer.


## What you can buy

The Azure Marketplace listing has two plans:

- **Enterprise license, LangWatch Cloud**: Enterprise on [LangWatch Cloud](https://app.langwatch.ai). After purchase we enable Enterprise on your organization.
- **Enterprise license, self-hosted**: an Enterprise license for LangWatch on your own infrastructure, with [Docker Compose](/self-hosting/deployment/docker-compose) or [Helm](/self-hosting/deployment/kubernetes-helm). See [Editions and licensing](/self-hosting/licensing) for what the license adds.

For [Hybrid](/hybrid-setup/overview), Cloud Enterprise on dedicated instances, or any other setup, [talk to sales](https://langwatch.ai/get-a-demo) about a private offer.

## After you buy

- **LangWatch Cloud**: we turn on Enterprise for your organization.
- **Self-hosted**: you receive an activation code or a signed license key. [Activate it](/self-hosting/licensing#activating-a-license) in **Settings → License**.
