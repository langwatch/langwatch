<Note>
  **Available on paid plans.** Configuring retention needs a paid plan. On the Free plan every project keeps data for the platform default of 49 days. Enterprise plans and self-hosted deployments with a license also get the longer presets and a custom period. Retained data over the plan volume is billed per extra GB, see the [pricing page](/pricing).
</Note>

## What retention covers

A retention policy sets a number of days for one scope. Data older than that is deleted. The policy covers three categories of data, and one policy sets the same period for all three:

| Category | Data |
|----------|------|
| Traces & Spans | Spans, log records, metrics, trace summaries, analytics, evaluation runs and Langy analytics events |
| Scenarios | Scenario runs and suite runs |
| Experiments | Experiment runs and their items |

Data stored in Postgres is not covered: annotations, datasets, prompts, and the settings pages keep their rows.

The [LangWatch AI Gateway spend records](/ai-gateway/billing-events) are outside retention. They keep a fixed 13 month lifetime, so you can reconcile invoices after the traces behind them are gone.

## Scopes

Write a policy at the organization, a team or a project. A project uses the most specific policy: its own, then its team's, then the organization's, then the platform default of 49 days.

## Periods

Periods are whole weeks. The presets round up so that the label is always covered:

| Preset | Days | Plans |
|--------|------|-------|
| 1 month | 35 | Paid, Enterprise, self-hosted |
| 2 months | 63 | Paid, Enterprise, self-hosted |
| 3 months | 91 | Enterprise, self-hosted |
| 1 year | 371 | Enterprise, self-hosted |
| 5 years | 1827 | Enterprise, self-hosted |
| Custom | 49 days or more, in weeks, months (28 days) or years (364 days), rounded to whole weeks | Enterprise, self-hosted |

The longest period is 65,534 days.

## Add a policy

Open **Settings > Data Retention**. Reading the page needs `project:view`. Writing a policy needs `organization:manage` for the organization, `team:manage` for a team and `project:update` for a project. The scope picker only offers the scopes you can write.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add retention policy">
    The drawer opens with the **Scope** picker and the **Retention** select.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one or more scopes">
    The picker is multi-select. One **Create** writes the same period to every scope you pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the retention period">
    Choose a preset, or **Custom...** on Enterprise and self-hosted, and enter an amount and a unit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide about existing data">
    **Apply this change to existing data** is off by default. Off: the period applies to data ingested from now on. On: LangWatch also restamps the rows already stored for the current project, and rows older than the new period become eligible for deletion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    The policy appears in the table. The row menu has **Edit**, which reopens the drawer with the scope locked and saves with **Save changes**, and **Remove**, after which the scope falls back to the next policy in the chain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deletion is permanent. Export data you need to keep before its period ends.
</Warning>

## How deletion runs

Every stored row carries its retention period. ClickHouse deletes expired rows during its background merges through a TTL expression, so a row disappears some time after its period ends, not at the exact second.

Shortening a period without **Apply this change to existing data** leaves stored rows on their old period. Lengthening a period never brings back deleted rows.

## Self-hosted deployments

Retention works the same way on your own ClickHouse. Files that traces reference in object storage, for example images, are not deleted by retention.
