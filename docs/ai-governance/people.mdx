Open **AI Governance > People** (`/governance/people`) to review people named by connected sources, match provider identities and manage departments for spend accounting. Departments change where spend is counted. They never grant or restrict access.

For access control, use [Access Control (RBAC)](/platform/rbac).

## What the People tab shows

The **People** tab lists people LangWatch knows about from directory data, connected providers and usage. Rows can include identity evidence, department assignment, source, spend and recent activity when those reads are available.

Use filters to narrow the list by department, source and match state. A missing value means LangWatch has not measured that value for the person yet, not that the value is zero.

## What the Departments tab shows

The **Departments** tab lists the active departments used for accounting. Create, rename and archive departments from this tab when you have `governance:manage`.

Archiving removes a department from pickers. It does not delete historical rows. Spend that no longer matches an active department is counted under **Unassigned**.

## How identity matching works

**Run match pass** asks LangWatch to review provider identities against known people. The pass creates suggestions where the evidence is strong enough to review, and it leaves uncertain identities unmatched instead of guessing.

Confirming a suggestion links the provider identity to the person for governance reporting. It does not create a login, change a role or grant access to anything.

## Assign departments

Assign departments from the People page, or from the organization settings pages that already list people, teams and projects.

| What | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| A person | **AI Governance > People**, or **Settings > Members** | The person's spend, including personal AI use, rolls up to this department |
| A team | **Settings > Teams & Projects** | Members and projects inherit this department when they have no department of their own |
| A project | **Settings > Teams & Projects** | Agent spend with no human principal rolls up to this department |

## How spend rolls up

Each request counts once, in exactly one department.

For a request made by a person, LangWatch uses the first department it can prove in this order: the person's department, the person's team department, then the project department. For a request with no person behind it, such as an autonomous agent, LangWatch uses the project department.

A request that matches none of these is counted under **Unassigned**. Read the department breakdown on [Costs](/ai-governance/costs).

## Assign departments through SCIM

When your identity provider provisions users through SCIM, LangWatch reads the `costCenter` attribute of the SCIM Enterprise User extension.

| The identity provider sends | LangWatch |
|---|---|
| A `costCenter` that matches an active department | Assigns the user to it |
| A `costCenter` that matches no active department | Creates the department and assigns the user |
| A new `costCenter` value on an update | Replaces the assignment |
| An empty `costCenter`, or removes the attribute | Clears the assignment |
| No `costCenter` in the request | Leaves the assignment as it is |

## Permissions

Reading People needs `governance:view`. Running a match pass, confirming a suggestion and changing departments need `governance:manage`.

Department assignment in **Settings > Members** and **Settings > Teams & Projects** needs the organization management permissions for those settings pages.

**Also check:** [Costs](/ai-governance/costs) for department spend, [SCIM](/platform/scim) for provisioning setup, and [Members and Teams](/platform/members-and-teams) for organization membership settings.
