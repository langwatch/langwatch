AI Governance is the part of LangWatch where platform and IT teams manage approved AI tools, connected activity sources, agent inventory, spend attribution and each developer's personal tool portal.

The section is in the sidebar under **AI Governance**. The main pages are **Overview**, **Costs**, **Inventory**, **Agents** and **People**. Opening the section needs `governance:view`, which an organization Admin holds by default.

## What the overview shows

The overview at `/governance` is the way into the section rather than a metrics dashboard. It opens with a greeting, a field for asking a question or jumping to a screen, **Add Source** for admins who can manage ingestion sources, and shortcuts to add people, an agent or a tool.

  ![The AI Governance overview](/images/ai-governance/governance-overview.png)
</Frame>

## What Inventory shows

Inventory has three tabs: **Catalog**, **Environments** and **Sources**.

**Catalog** holds the AI tool tiles that developers see on `/me`. Use it to publish coding assistants, model providers and external tools, then scope each tile to the organization or to departments.

<Frame caption="The Catalog tab after the starter pack import, with coding assistants and model providers scoped to the organization.">
  ![The Catalog tab of the inventory](/images/ai-governance/governance-inventory-catalog.png)
</Frame>

**Sources** lists the systems that report AI activity. A source can push events in real time, or LangWatch can pull from a provider API, S3 export or Dataverse table on a schedule.

<Frame caption="The Sources tab with connected feeds and their current status.">
  ![The Sources tab of the inventory](/images/ai-governance/governance-inventory-sources.png)
</Frame>

## What the personal home page shows

`/me` shows each developer the tools published for them, the budgets that apply to them and their usage this month.

</Frame>
